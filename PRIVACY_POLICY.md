# Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

**Last Updated / 最后更新**: 2024

## English Version

### Introduction

Duxiang.ai ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Chrome/Edge browser extension ("Extension") handles your information.

### Information We Collect

The Extension operates entirely locally on your device. We do NOT collect, transmit, or share any of your data with external servers or third parties.

**Data Stored Locally:**

- **Saved Links**: Titles, URLs, tags, timestamps, and pin status of links you save to your reading list
- **Browser Bookmarks** (if you choose to import): We only access your bookmarks when you explicitly click the "Import Bookmarks" button. This data is processed locally and added to your reading list.

**Browser Permissions:**

The Extension requires the following permissions to function:

- `activeTab`: To get the current page's title and URL when you save a link
- `tabs`: To create new tabs when opening saved links
- `storage`: For data migration from older versions (legacy support)
- `bookmarks`: To import your browser bookmarks when you request it
- `scripting`: For extension functionality
- `commands`: To support keyboard shortcuts

### How We Use Your Data

All data is stored locally on your device using IndexedDB. We use your data ONLY for:

- Displaying your saved reading list in the extension popup
- Enabling search functionality within your saved links
- Opening saved links in new browser tabs when you click them
- Importing bookmarks from your browser (only when you explicitly request it)

### Data Storage and Security

- **Local Storage Only**: All your data is stored locally on your device using IndexedDB
- **No Cloud Sync**: We do not sync, upload, or transmit your data to any servers
- **No Account Required**: You do not need to create an account or provide any personal information
- **Data Deletion**: You can delete individual links or all data through the extension interface. Clearing your browser data may also delete stored links.

### Third-Party Services

This Extension does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. It operates completely offline after installation.

### Your Rights

You have full control over your data:

- **View**: You can view all your saved links in the extension popup
- **Edit**: You can edit titles, URLs, and tags of any saved link
- **Delete**: You can delete individual links or export all data before clearing
- **Export**: You can export all your data as a JSON file at any time
- **Import**: You can import bookmarks from your browser or restore from exported JSON files

### Children's Privacy

This Extension is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

### Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last Updated" date at the top indicates when changes were last made. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

### Contact Me

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the email address notfresh@foxmail.com.

## 中文版本

### 介绍

读享收藏夹（"我们"或"我们的"）致力于保护您的隐私。本隐私政策说明了我们的 Chrome/Edge 浏览器扩展（"扩展"）如何处理您的信息。

### 我们收集的信息

本扩展完全在您的设备本地运行。我们**不会**收集、传输或与外部服务器或第三方共享您的任何数据。

**本地存储的数据：**

- **保存的链接**：您保存到阅读列表的链接的标题、URL、标签、时间戳和置顶状态
- **浏览器收藏夹**（如果您选择导入）：仅在您明确点击"导入收藏夹"按钮时，我们才会访问您的收藏夹。这些数据在本地处理并添加到您的阅读列表中。

**浏览器权限：**

本扩展需要以下权限才能正常工作：

- `activeTab`：在您保存链接时获取当前页面的标题和 URL
- `tabs`：在打开已保存链接时创建新标签页
- `storage`：用于从旧版本进行数据迁移（向后兼容支持）
- `bookmarks`：在您请求时导入浏览器收藏夹
- `scripting`：用于扩展功能
- `commands`：支持键盘快捷键

### 我们如何使用您的数据

所有数据都使用 IndexedDB 存储在您的设备本地。我们仅将您的数据用于：

- 在扩展弹出窗口中显示您保存的阅读列表
- 在您保存的链接中启用搜索功能
- 当您点击链接时在新浏览器标签页中打开保存的链接
- 从浏览器导入收藏夹（仅在您明确请求时）

### 数据存储和安全

- **仅本地存储**：您的所有数据都使用 IndexedDB 存储在您的设备本地
- **无云同步**：我们不会将您的数据同步、上传或传输到任何服务器
- **无需账户**：您无需创建账户或提供任何个人信息
- **数据删除**：您可以通过扩展界面删除单个链接或所有数据。清除浏览器数据也可能删除存储的链接。

### 第三方服务

本扩展不使用任何第三方分析、广告或跟踪服务。安装后完全离线运行。

### 您的权利

您对数据拥有完全控制权：

- **查看**：您可以在扩展弹出窗口中查看所有保存的链接
- **编辑**：您可以编辑任何已保存链接的标题、URL 和标签
- **删除**：您可以删除单个链接，或在清除前导出所有数据
- **导出**：您可以随时将所有数据导出为 JSON 文件
- **导入**：您可以从浏览器导入收藏夹或从导出的 JSON 文件恢复

### 儿童隐私

本扩展不适用于 13 岁以下的用户。我们不会故意收集儿童的信息。

### 隐私政策的变更

我们可能会不时更新本隐私政策。顶部的"最后更新"日期表示最后更改的时间。我们建议您定期查看此政策。

### 联系我

如果您对本隐私政策有任何疑问，请通过邮件联系我 notfresh@foxmail.com。
